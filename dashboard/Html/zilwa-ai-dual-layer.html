Dual-Layer AI — Super AI + Shadow AI

Doctrine sketch for a future guest/staff assist layer. No models run. No API calls.

Super AI (concept)

Human-visible assistant for cultural context, rest reminders, fair-value education copy — always labeled suggestive, never authoritative.

Cannot: book, pay, diagnose health, approve stories, or override stewards.

Shadow AI (concept)

Internal governance mirror — flags exploitation language, missing consent labels, or over-collection in drafts. Not surveillance of guests.

Cannot: run silently on live conversations in Phase 1A.

Locked law

AI concept ≠ live AI. Shadow layer ≠ spying on families. Super layer ≠ medical, legal, or financial advice.

Dual-layer AI doctrine

Concept only — not live — human/steward approval required.
