# ZILWA — Oral History Tech Bridge Policy

**Status:** Future concept only · **no uploads · no audio in Phase 1A**

---

## Purpose

If technology ever supports elder stories, it must **bridge** stewards and families — not bypass them.

---

## Family-first law

- Stories belong to elder and family first
- Metadata may show title, theme, region, consent status — **not story body** in public demo
- No default platform IP ownership
- No AI training on recordings without revocable family license
- Tech bridge ≠ automatic archive upload
