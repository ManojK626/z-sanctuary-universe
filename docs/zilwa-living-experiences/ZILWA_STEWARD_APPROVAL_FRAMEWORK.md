# ZILWA Steward Approval Framework

> Governance framework only. No automatic approvals. Every stage requires
> human review and sign-off.

## Framework Purpose

This framework defines the sequential review stages that any new ZILWA activity,
partnership, experience type, or programme change must pass before activation. It
exists to protect people, culture, community, and environment from well-intentioned
but under-reviewed decisions.

No stage may be skipped. No stage may be marked complete without genuine review.
Completeness is not the goal — thoroughness is.

## Core Law

> No ZILWA activity activates without a complete review chain and human gate sign-off.

This is unconditional. Commercial urgency, operational pressure, or stakeholder
enthusiasm do not override the review sequence.

---

## Stage 1: Community Review

Reviewers: Community representatives, including residents with direct proximity to the
proposed activity, local business voices, and civic stakeholders.

Review scope:

- Does the proposed activity respect community carrying capacity?
- Does it benefit the community fairly?
- Does it create unacceptable burden, noise, privacy intrusion, or congestion?
- Have residents with direct impact been consulted?

Required outcome: Written confirmation from community review participants that concerns
are resolved or documented for escalation.

HOLD condition: Any unresolved community objection blocks progression to Stage 2.

---

## Stage 2: Host-Family Review

Reviewers: Host families directly involved in the proposed activity or affected by its
operational footprint.

Review scope:

- Is the hosting load within sustainable limits under the Host Sustainability Charter?
- Do hosts understand and consent to all specific obligations?
- Are rest periods, privacy protections, and right-to-decline conditions confirmed?

Required outcome: Written confirmation from each involved host family that participation
terms are understood, voluntary, and acceptable.

HOLD condition: Any host family uncertainty, unresolved concern, or absence of confirmed
consent blocks progression to Stage 3.

---

## Stage 3: Elder Review

Reviewers: Elders or knowledge holders whose traditions, stories, or cultural practices
are involved in the proposed activity.

Review scope:

- Is the proposed use of cultural knowledge consistent with the Elder Rights Charter?
- Is consent informed, specific, and freely given?
- Are ownership protections clearly understood?
- Are there elements the elder considers private, sacred, or not for tourism purposes?

Required outcome: Written confirmation from each relevant knowledge holder that
participation is voluntary, consent is specific and informed, and no sacred or private
boundaries are being crossed.

HOLD condition: Any elder withdrawal, unresolved concern, or absence of specific consent
blocks progression to Stage 4.

---

## Stage 4: Accessibility Review

Reviewers: An accessibility-aware reviewer — qualified specialist preferred; at minimum
a representative familiar with guests with disabilities, mobility limitations, hearing or
visual impairments, or neurodivergent profiles.

Review scope:

- Is the proposed activity accessible across the profiles in the Accessibility Charter?
- Are accessible alternatives available where the primary format is not universal?
- Are communication, wayfinding, pacing, and transport arrangements inclusive?

Required outcome: Written confirmation that accessibility considerations have been
reviewed and either resolved or documented as planned improvements with a clear delivery
commitment.

HOLD condition: Any identified exclusion risk without a proposed solution blocks
progression to Stage 5.

---

## Stage 5: Legal Review

Reviewers: A qualified legal professional in the applicable jurisdiction.

Review scope:

- All applicable items in the Legal Readiness Checklist for the relevant activity class.
- Liability exposure, insurance adequacy, regulatory compliance.
- Specific review for child-facing activities, home dining, transport, and marine/reef
  activities as applicable.

Required outcome: Legal sign-off confirming all checklist items are confirmed compliant
or not applicable, with no outstanding items requiring further review.

HOLD condition: Any unresolved legal item is an unconditional HOLD. No exception.

---

## Stage 6: Human Gate Sign-Off

Sign-off authority: The accountable human authority designated for ZILWA governance.
Human only — not AI, not automated process.

Review scope:

- Completeness of all prior stages.
- Quality of evidence provided at each stage.
- Absence of any outstanding HOLD conditions.
- Alignment with the ZILWA Governance Review Sprint, Risk Register, and all charters.

Required outcome: Explicit written sign-off authorising the activity to proceed, with
conditions documented if applicable.

HOLD condition: Any incomplete prior stage, any outstanding concern, or any reviewer
expressing non-resolution is a HOLD. Human gate sign-off may not be delegated to any
automated or AI system.

---

## Framework Maintenance

This framework must be reviewed and updated when:

- A new activity class is proposed.
- Regulatory requirements change.
- Charter documents are updated.
- Lessons from incidents or near-misses require governance strengthening.

Minimum review interval: annually, or upon any significant programme change.
