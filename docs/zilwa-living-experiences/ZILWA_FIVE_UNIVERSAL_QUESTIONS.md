# ZILWA — Five Universal Listening Questions

**Phase:** 2B Steward Reality Review  
**Use:** Every persona · every conversation · optional opening before role-specific questions

Ask slowly. Allow silence. Do not argue. Do not sell ZILWA.

## The five questions

### 1. What are we doing well?

Capture genuine strengths — in tourism, hospitality, community life, or guest experience.

### 2. What is hurting people?

Name pain without blame where possible. Listen for patterns, not single incidents only.

### 3. What is disappearing that should be preserved?

Stories, skills, traditions, guest experiences, worker pathways, community practices.

### 4. What opportunity are we missing?

Ideas people see but no one has acted on — especially for workers, elders, families, communities.

### 5. If you could change one thing tomorrow, what would it be?

One change only. Often the clearest signal of priority.

## Facilitator notes

- **Simple questions. Powerful answers.** Do not over-explain ZILWA.
- Prefer **in-person or voice** where trust exists; never pressure participation.
- Follow [ZILWA_LISTENING_CONSENT_AND_SAFEGUARDING.md](ZILWA_LISTENING_CONSENT_AND_SAFEGUARDING.md).
- Record only with explicit consent; default is **steward handwritten notes**.
- Do **not** upload raw interviews to the repository without named consent and redaction review.
