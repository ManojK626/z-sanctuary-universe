# ZILWA Living Experiences — Steward Review Pack

**For:** Host families · Community leaders · Elders · Tourism stakeholders
**What this is:** A plain-language guide to review a **demonstration exhibit** — not a live booking app.
**Your role:** Tell us what feels respectful, what feels risky, and what must never happen in real life.

---

## Project summary

**ZILWA Living Experiences** is a vision for **community-connected hospitality in Mauritius** — where guests stay with host families, meet cultural ambassadors, learn about reef care, and may be invited (with consent) to hear elder stories.

Right now, ZILWA exists only as:

- Written principles (how we promise to behave)
- Six **museum-style screen mockups** (pictures of what it _could_ look like)

There is **no live system**. No bookings. No payments. No guest lists. No health forms. No recordings.

---

## Objectives

| What we hope for | What we are not doing yet |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Fair treatment of host families | Forcing hosts to accept more guests than they want |
| Respect for elders and their stories | Taking ownership of family stories |
| Cultural exchange with dignity | Turning culture into a performance for tourists |
| Reef and beach education | Claiming scientific proof or official endorsement |
| Community-rooted journeys | Privatising public beaches, buses, or markets |

---

## Benefits (if done right, in the future)

- **Host families** may receive fair recognition and rest, not endless hosting pressure.
- **Guests** may learn Mauritius through real homes and real courtesy — not only hotels.
- **Ambassadors** may help translation and context without speaking for whole communities.
- **Elders** may choose whether to share stories — families keep control.
- **Youth** may learn stewardship of land and sea.
- **Local communities** may benefit from transparent give-back ideas (still only illustrated today).

---

## Risks we already know about

| Risk | Why it matters |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Mock screens look “too real” | People may think they can book today |
| Star ratings on ambassadors | May feel like pressure or unfair judging |
| Capacity bars for host homes | May feel like quotas or income targets |
| Money percentages shown | May feel like a promise of payment |
| Story “archive” title | May sound like stories are stored by a company |
| Reef numbers on a dashboard | May look like official science or government data |
| Using public bus and market in tours | Must not disrespect daily life or crowd local spaces |

---

## Protections already written into the vision

- **Demonstration labels** on every screen: not live, no payments, no health data, no guest records.
- **Elder stories:** titles and themes only — **story content is not shown** in the demo.
- **Host rest days** and **capacity limits** are described as dignity guards, not sales targets.
- **Money figures** are labeled _illustrative only_ — not real invoices.
- **No audio, no uploads, no tracking** in this phase.
- **Human approval required** before any next step toward a real app.

---

## The six demonstration screens (what to look at)

1. **Guest journey** — A fictional guest’s week (arrival, meal, bus, reef walk, elder invitation, goodbye).
2. **Ambassador profiles** — Fictional names, languages, and demo ratings.
3. **Host family panel** — Capacity, rest day, fair-share reminder, safety reminders.
4. **Elder story cards** — Metadata only; content locked.
5. **Reef awareness** — Education numbers marked as illustrative.
6. **Community journeys** — Market walk, village visit, bus ride, low-tide walk — information only.

**How to open:** Ask the ZILWA team to show these on a laptop or projector. Entry screen: Guest Journey mockup.

---

## Review questions

### For host families

1. Does the host panel feel supportive — or does it pressure you to take more guests?
2. Is the rest-day idea clear and respected?
3. Would the fair-share percentage create false hope or confusion?
4. What would you need to see before trusting any future real system?

### For community leaders

1. Do the journey ideas (market, village, bus) respect public life — or commodify it?
2. Who should approve new experiences in your area?
3. What would exploitation look like — and how should ZILWA block it?

### For elders and families

1. Does “story archive” feel safe — or does it sound like stories belong to a platform?
2. Is “story content not displayed” enough protection in a demo?
3. What consent process would you require before any recording ever exists?

### For tourism stakeholders

1. Does this complement regulated tourism — or blur lines that need licenses?
2. What legal or tourism-board conversations should happen before any pilot?
3. Should bilingual (Kreol / French / English) copy come before any public showing?

### For everyone

1. What is missing from this vision?
2. What should **never** be built?
3. Would you be willing to join a small steward circle for the next review round?

---

## What happens after your review

Your answers are **not** collected by these web pages. Notes should be taken in person or on paper by the project steward.

Possible outcomes:

- **Continue** — vision aligned; prepare printable exhibit and community workshops.
- **Adjust** — change words, remove screens, or add warnings.
- **Pause** — do not show publicly until concerns are addressed.

**No automatic next step.** AMK-Goku and human stewards decide.

## Contact posture

This pack is for **conversation**, not signup. There is no form, no database, and no guest list on these demonstration pages.
