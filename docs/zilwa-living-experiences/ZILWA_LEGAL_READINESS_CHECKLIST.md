# ZILWA Legal Readiness Checklist

> **Checklist only. This is not legal advice. Every item requires review by a
> qualified legal professional in the applicable jurisdiction before activation.**

## Checklist Purpose

This checklist identifies the legal and regulatory readiness questions that must be
answered before each ZILWA activity class can be activated. No item on this checklist
is trivial. A "not yet confirmed" status on any critical item is a HOLD signal.

## How to Use

- Review each category before the relevant activity class is considered for launch.
- Mark each item: Confirmed / Pending / Not Applicable / Requires Legal Review.
- Items marked "Requires Legal Review" must be resolved with qualified legal counsel.
- This checklist must be reviewed and refreshed at each significant programme change.

---

## 1. Tourism Regulatory

- [ ] Tourism operator licensing requirements confirmed for Mauritius.
- [ ] Applicable tourism authority registration or notification completed.
- [ ] Tour guide licensing or certification requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Consumer protection obligations for tourism services confirmed.
- [ ] Applicable tourism standards or codes of practice identified.

---

## 2. Transportation

- [ ] Duty-of-care obligations for guest transport confirmed.
- [ ] Applicable vehicle safety and roadworthiness requirements confirmed.
- [ ] Driver licensing and vetting requirements confirmed.
- [ ] Insurance requirements for guest-carrying vehicles confirmed.
- [ ] Liability framework for transport-related incidents confirmed.

---

## 3. Hospitality (General Guest Services)

- [ ] Host-family hospitality obligations under Mauritius law confirmed.
- [ ] Guest registration or reporting obligations confirmed.
- [ ] Health and safety obligations for guest-facing premises confirmed.
- [ ] Insurance and liability coverage for hosted experiences confirmed.
- [ ] Applicable hospitality-sector standards reviewed.

---

## 4. Home Dining

- [ ] Food safety and hygiene licensing requirements for home/community dining confirmed.
- [ ] Food handler certification requirements confirmed.
- [ ] Allergen disclosure and management obligations confirmed.
- [ ] Liability exposure for home-prepared food for paying guests confirmed.
- [ ] Health inspection obligations confirmed.

---

## 5. Environmental Activities (Reef, Beach, Marine)

- [ ] Protected area designations for intended experience locations confirmed.
- [ ] Permits or authorisations required for guided beach/reef/marine activities confirmed.
- [ ] Environmental protection obligations for low-tide and reef-adjacent activities confirmed.
- [ ] Marine activity licensing or notification requirements confirmed.
- [ ] Environmental impact assessment obligations reviewed.

---

## 6. Child Safeguarding

- [ ] Background screening obligations for child-facing roles confirmed.
- [ ] Mandatory reporting obligations for child safeguarding concerns confirmed.
- [ ] Applicable child protection legislation identified and reviewed.
- [ ] Insurance and liability for child-facing activities confirmed.
- [ ] Regulatory expectations for kids-club or family-programme operations confirmed.

---

## 7. Privacy and Data

- [ ] Data protection legislation applicable to ZILWA operations identified.
- [ ] Obligations for any guest information collected confirmed.
- [ ] Health-related information handling obligations confirmed and minimisation
      approach validated.
- [ ] Photography and media consent obligations confirmed.
- [ ] Data retention and deletion obligations confirmed.

---

## 8. Insurance

- [ ] Public liability insurance requirements confirmed for all activity classes.
- [ ] Employer/contractor liability insurance requirements confirmed.
- [ ] Specific coverage for child-facing activities confirmed.
- [ ] Specific coverage for water/marine activities confirmed.
- [ ] Specific coverage for transport confirmed.
- [ ] Coverage adequacy reviewed by qualified insurance professional.

---

## Checklist Completion Gate

Before any ZILWA activity class is operationally activated, the relevant section of
this checklist must be completed with "Confirmed" or "Not Applicable" status for all
items. Any remaining "Requires Legal Review" item is a hard HOLD.

Human gate sign-off is required on completed sections before activation.
