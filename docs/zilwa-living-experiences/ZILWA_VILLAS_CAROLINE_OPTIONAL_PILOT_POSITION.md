# ZILWA — Villas Caroline Optional Pilot Position

**Posture:** Concept only · **AMBER+ HOLD** · not a commercial commitment

## Position

**Villas Caroline** may serve as a **possible pilot hospitality partner** for early ZILWA static-to-steward walkthroughs in Mauritius. It is **not**:

- The sole owner of ZILWA
- A single point of failure for the global model
- An automatic live deployment target
- A payment, booking, or data integration in Phase 2A

## What a pilot could mean (future — gated)

- Host-family and ambassador walkthroughs with steward sign-off
- Static exhibit review on property (offline HTML)
- Feedback from staff, guests, and community stewards
- Optional co-branding only with written consent

## What it does not mean

- Exclusive franchise or trademark lock-in
- Health passport, child data, or guest record storage
- Live AI concierge or automated decisions
- Reef, weather, or safety authority claims

## Global model independence

ZILWA 2A explicitly states: **Mauritius as possible origin pilot, not dependency.** Villas Caroline is one optional partner among many future community anchors.

## Approval

Any live pilot requires **AMK + steward + community consent** before operations.
