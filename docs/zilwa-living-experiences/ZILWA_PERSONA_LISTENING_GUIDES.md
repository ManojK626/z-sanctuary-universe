# ZILWA — Persona Listening Guides (Phase 2B)

**Posture:** Listening only · no ZILWA pitch required · AMK+ HOLD

Start every session with [ZILWA_FIVE_UNIVERSAL_QUESTIONS.md](ZILWA_FIVE_UNIVERSAL_QUESTIONS.md).  
Add [ZILWA_LOST_KNOWLEDGE_LANE.md](ZILWA_LOST_KNOWLEDGE_LANE.md) when the person has long memory of tourism or community life.

---

## Tourism worker (active)

**Opening:** *We are listening to people who have lived tourism — not selling anything today.*

| Question | Listen for |
| -------- | ---------- |
| What frustrates you most in your work today? | Recognition gaps, pressure, unfair treatment |
| What would improve your future? | Training, stability, respect, career paths |
| What recognition is missing? | Titles, visibility, retirement honor, mentorship |
| What should **never** happen? | Exploitation lines, safety boundaries |
| What did supervisors or long-serving colleagues teach you that newcomers never learn? | Lost knowledge |

**Hypotheses to validate (not lead):** recognition, future security, retirement, training, respect.

---

## Retired tourism worker

**Opening:** *Your experience matters — especially what younger workers may never hear.*

| Question | Listen for |
| -------- | ---------- |
| What knowledge is disappearing? | Skills, customs, guest care, craft |
| What should younger workers learn? | Mentorship priorities |
| What would make retirement more meaningful? | Legacy roles, honor, part-time teaching |
| What used to make this industry feel proud? | Lost dignity signals |
| What would you want Villas Caroline — or any employer — to understand? | Optional; no commitment implied |

---

## Parent (local or visiting)

**Opening:** *We want to understand what families need — especially around holidays and children.*

| Question | Listen for |
| -------- | ---------- |
| What would make holidays easier? | Cost, logistics, trust, language |
| What would make children safer? | Supervision, activities, boundaries |
| Which experiences matter most for your family? | Culture, nature, rest, learning |
| What worries you about tourism near your community? | Crowding, safety, values |
| What would you never allow your child to join without proof of safety? | Hard lines for 3A child policy |

---

## Tourist / guest

**Opening:** *What memories stayed with you — and what felt real or fake?*

| Question | Listen for |
| -------- | ---------- |
| What memories stayed with you from Mauritius (or similar destinations)? | Emotional anchors |
| What felt authentic? | Human connection, local life |
| What felt artificial? | Performative culture, poverty tourism, isolation in resorts |
| Did you understand the place beyond the hotel? | Gap ZILWA may address — or not |
| What would you tell a friend to seek — or avoid? | Honest guest voice |

---

## Community member / leader

**Opening:** *Tourism affects communities — we want to hear benefits and harms honestly.*

| Question | Listen for |
| -------- | ---------- |
| What benefits does tourism bring here? | Jobs, pride, exchange, projects |
| What harms does tourism bring? | Cost of living, crowding, cultural pressure |
| What is missing for local people? | Revenue leakage, opportunity gaps |
| Who is not being heard? | Workers, elders, youth, fishers, market vendors |
| What would fair tourism look like from your view? | Community-defined success |

---

## Elder (dedicated lane)

Combine **community** questions with **lost knowledge** and:

| Question | Listen for |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Am I respected in how tourism uses our stories? | Dignity, consent |
| Am I being used — or truly valued? | Exploitation vs honor |
| May I choose what is shared and what stays private? | Consent boundaries |
| Who in my family must approve before anything is recorded? | Family governance |

See also [ZILWA_ELDER_ORAL_HISTORY_POLICY.md](ZILWA_ELDER_ORAL_HISTORY_POLICY.md) — policy applies to future phases; 2B is listen-only.
