# ZILWA — Staff & Cultural Ambassador Code of Conduct

**Status:** Doctrine only · Phase 1A static · **not enforced by software**

---

## Purpose

Dignity-first rules for anyone representing ZILWA in a **future** live phase. Phase 1A displays these as reference cards only.

---

## Core commitments

- Protect host families from over-hosting and unfair splits
- Never speak for an entire community or elder without consent
- No photography of children for promotion without guardian approval
- No pressure on elders to perform stories
- Respect public beaches and markets — no extractive or privatizing behavior
- Escalate exploitation concerns to **human stewards** — not AI-only resolution

---

## Forbidden (now and in future without charter)

- Hidden fees or undisclosed ambassador commissions
- Health or fitness claims without medical gate
- Political campaigning as "culture"
- Recording oral histories without family license
- Live AI sending messages to guests without human approve
