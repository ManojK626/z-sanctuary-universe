## Permanent Prohibition Candidates

1. No child-facing activity without licensed and verified safeguarding controls.
2. No health data/passport handling beyond minimum lawful, documented necessity.
3. No elder-knowledge commercialization without explicit consent and compensation agreement.
4. No sensitive environmental activity without ecological threshold controls.

## HOLD Gate Conditions

Keep initiative in HOLD until all below are true:

- ZILWA doctrine pack exists and is internally approved.
- Community rights and burden-sharing controls are documented.
- Legal reviews are completed for dining, transport, child/family, and marine activities.
- Accessibility standards are codified and tested at concept level.
- Risk ownership matrix assigns accountable role for every critical risk.

## Residual Risk Outlook

Even with controls, long-term risk remains **moderate to high** because the model blends intimate community interaction with regulated tourism operations. Governance depth, pace discipline, and independent oversight determine viability.

## Register Verdict

**AMBER** with strict HOLD controls; escalates to **RED** if governance artifacts remain absent while operational ambition increases.
