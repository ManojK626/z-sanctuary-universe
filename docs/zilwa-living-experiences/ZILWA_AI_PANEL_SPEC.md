# ZILWA LIVING EXPERIENCES — AI Panel Spec (Phase 0)

**System ID:** ZILWA-0
**Status:** Spec only — **no live AI** in Phase 0
**Hub:** Z-Sanctuary Universe

---

## Purpose

This document specifies a **future AI assistance panel** for ZILWA hosts, ambassadors, and operators. Phase 0 defines **roles, boundaries, and HOLD** — no LLM runtime, no external AI APIs, no autonomous actions.

---

## Human gate

**Human Gate Required Before Phase 1.**

Live AI requires AMK gate plus [Z-GITHUB-AI-COMMS-PRECAUTIONS.md](../Z-GITHUB-AI-COMMS-PRECAUTIONS.md) alignment and explicit **no sacred-move** automation.

## Panel roles (doctrine)

| Role | Allowed posture (future) | Forbidden |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cultural guide** | Suggest respectful greetings, customs context | Stereotyping; speaking for elders without consent |
| **Itinerary helper** | Draft local activity ideas | Booking without human confirm |
| **Host support** | Rest, capacity, fair-pricing reminders | Pressure to over-host |
| **Guest orientation** | House rules, safety copy | Health diagnosis; legal advice |
| **Escalation router** | Point to human steward | Autonomous dispute resolution |

## Privacy and safety

- **No** training on elder oral histories without explicit family license
- **No** child-directed profiling or chat without guardian gate
- **No** health interpretation — health passport remains future concept
- **No** silent logging of guest conversations to third-party AI vendors without charter
