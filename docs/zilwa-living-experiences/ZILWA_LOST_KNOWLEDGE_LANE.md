# ZILWA — Lost Knowledge Lane

**Phase:** 2B Steward Reality Review  
**Purpose:** Recover forgotten wisdom — often richer than inventing new technology

## Why this lane exists

Most tourism innovation starts with investors, managers, and executives. ZILWA can start with **workers, families, communities, and elders** — including what **used to exist** and no longer does.

Breakthroughs often come from **recovering lost wisdom**, not adding apps.

## Questions — Lost Knowledge Lane

Ask in addition to the [five universal questions](ZILWA_FIVE_UNIVERSAL_QUESTIONS.md):

1. **What used to exist that no longer exists?**  
   Services, rituals, guest experiences, worker roles, community gatherings.

2. **What traditions disappeared?**  
   Food, music, language use, seasonal practices, intergenerational teaching.

3. **What guest experiences were once common?**  
   How visitors connected with locals before mass-resort patterns.

4. **What skills are not being passed down?**  
   Fishing, gardening, crafts, hospitality crafts, oral history, boat work, cooking.

5. **What made tourism feel more human?**  
   Specific memories — not nostalgia for exploitation, but dignity and connection.

## Who to ask

| Source | May hold |
| ------ | -------- |
| Retired tourism workers | Institutional memory |
| Elders | Pre-tourism and early-tourism community life |
| Long-serving staff | What changed on the job |
| Community leaders | What tourism took or gave |
| Returning guests | What they remember from decades ago |

## Safeguards

- **No romanticizing poverty** or hardship as “authentic.”
- **Credit knowledge holders** — stories belong to people and families.
- **No recording** without consent; see safeguarding doc.
- Findings inform **3A impact themes** and **2A module priorities** — not immediate product builds.
