# ZILWA — Worker Dignity and Legacy Network

**Posture:** Doctrine only · **AMBER+ HOLD**

## Commitment

ZILWA honors **workers who built tourism** — fishermen, gardeners, cleaners, cooks, boat captains, retired hotel staff, artisans, and elders — as **masters of life**, not props.

## Principles

| Principle | Application                                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fair pay  | Illustrative models only until live charter             |
| Consent   | Masters choose what to teach and how they are portrayed |
| Legacy    | Retired workers can mentor without exploitation         |
| Credit    | Names and stories belong to workers and families        |
| Rest      | No pressure to perform when unwell or unpaid            |

## Anti-patterns (blocked)

- Poverty tourism or staged hardship
- Unpaid “cultural performances”
- Knowledge extraction without compensation
- Filming workers without consent

## Gate

Live workshops require **written consent, fair-pay charter, and steward review**.
