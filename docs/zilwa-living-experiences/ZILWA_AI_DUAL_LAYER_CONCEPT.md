# ZILWA — Dual-Layer AI Concept (Super AI + Shadow AI)

**Status:** Future concept only · **no live AI in Phase 1A**

---

## Super AI (guest/host-visible concept)

Suggestive assistant for cultural context, rest reminders, fair-value education — **never** authoritative for health, law, or payments.

## Shadow AI (governance concept)

Draft-review mirror for exploitation language, missing consent labels, over-collection — **not** guest surveillance.
