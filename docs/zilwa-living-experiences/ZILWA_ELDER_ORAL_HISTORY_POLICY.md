# ZILWA LIVING EXPERIENCES — Elder Oral History Policy

**System ID:** ZILWA-0
**Status:** Policy doctrine only
**Hub:** Z-Sanctuary Universe

---

## Purpose

Elders carry **oral histories** — family, village, migration, labor, cyclone memory, and cultural knowledge. ZILWA must **preserve elder and family ownership** of these stories. The platform is a **steward**, not the default owner of narrative IP.

---

## Core ownership law

```text
Oral histories belong to the elder and their family first.
ZILWA does not claim platform ownership by default.
Recording, sharing, or AI use requires explicit, revocable consent.
```

---

## Consent layers (future operations)

| Layer | Requirement |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Elder assent** | Clear explanation in language elder prefers |
| **Family gate** | Where family custom requires collective consent |
| **Scope** | Personal memory vs community-sensitive knowledge |
| **Medium** | Audio, video, text — each separately consented |
| **Duration** | Time-limited license unless perpetual explicitly chosen |
| **Revocation** | Family may withdraw — takedown path human-operated |

---

## Forbidden practices

| Practice | Status |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Recording without knowledge | **FORBIDDEN** |
| Selling elder stories to third parties | **FORBIDDEN** without family license |
| Training AI on oral histories (default) | **FORBIDDEN** in Phase 0 and without family license |
| “Verified history” badges | **FORBIDDEN** — ZILWA is not an archive authority |
| Pressure on frail elders for content | **FORBIDDEN** |

---

## Sensitive knowledge

Some knowledge is **not for guests** — sacred knowledge, burial sites, private grief, or community-guarded craft secrets. Ambassadors and hosts must respect **closed doors** without penalty.

---

## Compensation

If future phases offer stipends for storytelling, terms must be **transparent and voluntary**. Illustrative ledger lines in [ZILWA_FINANCIAL_LEDGER_MOCK_SPEC.md](ZILWA_FINANCIAL_LEDGER_MOCK_SPEC.md) are **projections only** — not live payment.

---

## Archival partnership (future HOLD)

Partnership with museums or archives requires **family-led** agreements — not platform bulk upload. Phase 0: no archive integration.

---

## Child and elder intersection

When elders and children appear together in family contexts, apply [ZILWA_HEALTH_PRIVACY_AND_CHILD_SAFETY.md](ZILWA_HEALTH_PRIVACY_AND_CHILD_SAFETY.md) — guardian consent for minors.

## Human gate

**Human Gate Required Before Phase 1.**

Any mock UI showing “oral history cards” must use **synthetic placeholder** content only — no real elder recordings without consent workflow.
