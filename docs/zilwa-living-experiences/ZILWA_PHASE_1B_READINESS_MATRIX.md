## Open lane detail (Phase 1B — stewardship only)

### Bilingual content

- **Goal:** Same demonstration screens with respectful Kreol and French companion copy.
- **Risk:** Words that sound official or promise services.
- **Gate:** Community readers + steward sign-off before print or presentation.

### Printable exhibit pack

- **Goal:** Paper or PDF walkthrough for elders and hosts without requiring a website.
- **Risk:** PDF circulated without “demonstration only” cover page.
- **Gate:** Cover page mandatory; steward distributes.

### Public presentations

- **Goal:** Show vision; collect spoken feedback — no signup forms.
- **Risk:** Audience believes homestay is bookable today.
- **Gate:** Verbal + slide disclaimer at start and end; steward presents.

### Community workshops

- **Goal:** Structured listening sessions using [ZILWA_STEWARD_REVIEW_PACK.md](ZILWA_STEWARD_REVIEW_PACK.md) questions.
- **Risk:** Perceived data collection — use paper notes, not app.
- **Gate:** Facilitator trained on “no records stored.”

## Prerequisites before any Open item ships

1. Phase 1A merged or explicitly blessed on branch after human review.
2. Steward review pack circulated; at least one session planned or completed.
3. Audit findings (guest/host/elder/community/regulator lenses) addressed or accepted with documented exceptions.
4. AMK-Goku human gate for Phase 1B charter.
