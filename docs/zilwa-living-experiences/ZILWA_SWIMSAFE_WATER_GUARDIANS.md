# Z-SWIMSAFE — Swimming Teacher & Water Guardian Network

**Phase:** ZILWA 2A · Global Network Expansion  
**Posture:** Concept only · **AMBER+ HOLD** · not operational  
**Exhibit:** [zilwa-swimsafe-water-guardians.html](../../dashboard/Html/zilwa-swimsafe-water-guardians.html)

## Purpose

- Personal swimming lessons with certified instructors
- Family and small-group swimming sessions
- Supervised outdoor child activities near water
- Lagoon and beach safety awareness education
- Junior ocean guardian certificate concept (printable static only)

## Safety boundaries

- Not daycare or unsupervised childcare
- Certified adults required for any future live phase
- Child-safety policy and steward sign-off before operations
- No live booking, tracking, or child profiles

## Required safety wording

- Concept only — not live — human/steward approval required.
- No child data storage. No live supervision system. Certified human guardians required before any live phase.
- Not official safety, medical, marine, or meteorological advice.

## Live-phase gates (future — not in scope)

Human/steward approval, regional adaptation, and official partner review required before any runtime, booking, payment, or data storage.
