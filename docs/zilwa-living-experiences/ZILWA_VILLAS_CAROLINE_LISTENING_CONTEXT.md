# ZILWA — Villas Caroline Listening Context (Phase 2B)

**Posture:** Human signal captured · **not** a pilot commitment · **AMBER+ HOLD**

## Why this document exists

A visit to Villas Caroline surfaced something more valuable than a management pitch deck:

**Real-world human signal** — former colleagues, supervisors, long-serving workers, people who lived tourism.

Management can describe what a business wants. **Workers often describe what reality feels like.** ZILWA must bridge both — and **listen to workers before proposing**.

## What we are not doing in 2B

- Rushing a Villas Caroline proposal  
- Rushing Mauritius tourism officials  
- Announcing a pilot partnership  
- Recording names in the public repository  
- Implying worker feedback endorses ZILWA technology  

## What we are doing

- Structured listening using [ZILWA_STEWARD_REALITY_REVIEW_2B.md](ZILWA_STEWARD_REALITY_REVIEW_2B.md)  
- Validating hypotheses about recognition, legacy, retirement dignity, training, respect  
- Honoring that **concern from former colleagues about recognition and long-term dignity** may be one of the strongest pillars of the ecosystem  

## Relationship to optional pilot doc

[ZILWA_VILLAS_CAROLINE_OPTIONAL_PILOT_POSITION.md](ZILWA_VILLAS_CAROLINE_OPTIONAL_PILOT_POSITION.md) remains **concept only** — possible partner, not single point of failure.

**2B listening precedes any 4A proposal** that mentions Villas Caroline by name.

## Suggested listening order (not mandatory)

1. Long-serving and retired workers (including former colleagues)  
2. Supervisors who understand daily reality  
3. Community and elder voices near tourism corridors  
4. Parents and guests — only after worker dignity themes are understood  
5. Management — **after** worker reality is documented in steward synthesis  

## Statement we aim to earn for 4A

> *This was not designed in isolation. It was shaped by workers, elders, families, communities, and tourism stakeholders.*

That sentence is **earned in 2B**, not asserted in slides.

## Gate

AMK + steward approval before any named proposal, co-branding, or on-property pilot walkthrough beyond private listening.
