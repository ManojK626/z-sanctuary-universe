# ZILWA — Mauritius Tourism Officials Proposal Prep

**Posture:** Preparation document only · **AMBER+ HOLD** · not submitted automatically

## Purpose

Prepare a **respectful, evidence-based** proposal frame for Mauritius tourism stakeholders — without claiming operational authority or live deployment.

## Proposal themes (static)

1. **Human-centered tourism** — hosts, elders, masters, and guests as equals in dignity
2. **Community benefit** — fair pay, no poverty tourism, local knowledge protection
3. **Marine and lagoon stewardship** — education, not enforcement claims
4. **Child and elder safeguarding** — certified guardians, consent, no data storage in 2A
5. **Global replication with Mauritian roots** — origin pilot, not export monopoly

## What we are not proposing in Phase 2A

- Live booking platforms or payment rails
- Government endorsement implied by static HTML
- Carbon credits, reef restoration metrics without evidence
- AI automation or guest surveillance
