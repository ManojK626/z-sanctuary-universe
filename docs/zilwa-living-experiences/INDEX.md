## Overview

ZILWA is the Z-Sanctuary Universe module for **living world architecture** — a museum-style
blueprint presenting 20 dimensions of integrated human living experience. Each exhibit
represents a domain of life that deserves sanctuary: calm, intentional, and compassion-first.

> _Every living experience deserves a sanctuary — visible, honoured, and gently held._

- **No** database or health/passport data storage
- **No** escrow, payment, or financial logic
- **No** live AI inference or provider calls
- **No** deployment pipeline triggers
- **No** merge without AMK + steward approval
