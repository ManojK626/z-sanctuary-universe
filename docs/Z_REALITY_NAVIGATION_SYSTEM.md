# Z-Reality Navigation System (RNS)

**Formal ID:** **Z-RNS-ARCH-1** (umbrella doctrine — docs + registry only)
**Date:** 2026-05-21
**Authority:** AMK-Goku / human review — **not** legal advice

---

## One-line purpose

**Human clarity amplification** — a governed, local-first platform that helps people stay aware, organised, calm, and evidence-disciplined while navigating real-life complexity.

This is **not** “AI law replacement.” It is a **Reality Navigation System**: process understanding, documentation habits, emotional regulation support, and ethical action — with humans and qualified professionals in charge.

## What we teach (utility over hype)

- Awareness and organisation under stress
- Calm, evidence-first documentation
- Process maps and terminology (**not** legal advice)
- Timeline discipline and contradiction spotting
- Emotional regulation and ethical action habits
- Consent-based sharing and export control
