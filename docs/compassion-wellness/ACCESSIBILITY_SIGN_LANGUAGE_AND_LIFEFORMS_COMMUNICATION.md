# Accessibility, Sign Language, and Lifeforms Communication

**Phase:** 0 — Inclusion design (no live interpretation runtime)

---

## Purpose

Ensure **nonverbal**, **Deaf**, **hard of hearing**, **elder**, **disabled**, and **multilingual** users — and caregivers observing animals — can communicate **safety and comfort** clearly.

---

## Sign language support (concept)

- Link to qualified human interpreters for high-stakes sessions (future charter)
- UI reserves space for **sign-language video** resources (educational, not replacing interpreter where law requires)
- Session agreements available in signed video copies where operators provide them

---

## Gesture-based commands

Universal comfort signals (customizable per user):

| Signal              | Meaning                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Open palm stop**  | Stop immediately               |
| **Pause**           | Hold position, check in        |
| **Reduce pressure** | Lighter touch now              |
| **Water**           | Request drink break            |
| **Discomfort**      | Change position or end session |
| **Thumbs up**       | Continue as is                 |

Practitioners trained to honor signals over verbal politeness.

---

## Multilingual support

- UI and forms in multiple languages (future phases)
- Plain-language summaries at low reading level
- Avoid idioms in safety-critical copy

---

## User groups

| Group               | Accommodations                                                |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Nonverbal users** | Gesture deck, AAC integration hook (future)                   |
| **Elder users**     | Large text, high contrast, slower pacing defaults             |
| **Disabled users**  | Adjustable session position, equipment access notes           |
| **Children**        | Guardian consent; age-appropriate boundaries (future charter) |

---

## Animal body-language observation

**Clear statements:**

- AI does **not** magically speak to animals
- AI may help caregivers **structure notes** about observable cues (tail position, hiding, appetite log)
- Observations are **not** medical or behavioral certainty
- Licensed trainers or veterinarians remain authority for health and aggression risk

---

## AI limits in this layer

- No automated sign translation presented as certified legal interpretation
- No emotion certainty from faces or voices
- Accessibility AI = **layout, captions, reminders** — not judgment
