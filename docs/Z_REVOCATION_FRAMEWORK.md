## Why locked

Revocation is the **inverse of citizenship**.

Only after citizenship rules exist can you say: “This condition invalidates citizenship.”  
Otherwise revocation becomes arbitrary.

Lifecycle states on the Manifest (Suspended / Retired) are **placeholders** — not a revocation policy.

**Do not** expand this document until Citizenship Rules can name invalidating conditions with Evidence.
