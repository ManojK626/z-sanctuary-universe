## 1. The missing market (precise answer)

Months of ecosystem work asked: *What is missing in AI?*

A sharper answer for the Lifeboat horizon:

> The missing market is not another AI model.
> It is **Stewardship Engineering**.

Many specialize in AI governance, cybersecurity, backup, DR, compliance, enterprise architecture, or digital transformation.

Few ask the overarching question:

> Will this organization still be coherent, recoverable, and trustworthy in 20 years?

That is the space Project Lifeboat may eventually occupy — **only** after internal proof.

## 2. Discipline definition

**Stewardship Engineering** is not “backup consulting” and not “AI governance” alone.

**Mission:**

> Design organizations that can evolve, survive disruption, preserve knowledge, and recover with evidence.

Technology-agnostic: AI · IT · government · healthcare · universities · manufacturing · NGOs · family businesses.
