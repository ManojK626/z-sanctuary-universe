## Permanent sentences

### Decision law

> Every preservation decision shall be evidence-led, recovery-tested, migration-aware, and steward-reviewed.

### Framework law

> Every preservation framework shall outlive the technologies it evaluates.

### Steward's Axiom (guiding · not Book law)

> Stewardship is the discipline of making tomorrow's decisions understandable to those who were not here today.

### Era IV doctrine (Lifeboat Hall)

> Extraordinary technology eventually becomes ordinary. Extraordinary stewardship remains extraordinary.

## The Lifeboat Cycle

```text
Observe → Research → Evaluate → Decide → Validate → Preserve → Review → Repeat
```

Vendorless — so it survives technological change.

---

## The defining question

> Can you protect the future better than what we already trust?

New technology is not adopted for novelty.  
Unfamiliar technology is not rejected for unfamiliarity.  
Both must answer that question with **evidence**.

---

## What we evaluate

Project Lifeboat evaluates **stewardship ecosystems**, not storage SKUs alone.

| Not only | Also |
| --- | --- |
| A bucket product | Tooling · restore workflows · APIs · maturity · migration |
| A cloud brand | Operational experience · monitoring · hardware lifecycle (when appliance-tied) |

## Phase 0 milestone ladder

```text
Core trio research (B2 · R2 · C2)
        ↓
Framework review (Has the model become stable?)
        ↓
Evidence Framework Proven → Stewardship Framework Stable
        ↓
```

The real prize is **not** “Use B2 forever.”  
It is a **repeatable framework** that can evaluate any preservation ecosystem — today or twenty years from now.
