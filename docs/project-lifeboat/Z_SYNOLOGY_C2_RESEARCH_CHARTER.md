## Discipline (same as B2 and R2)

Not:

> It’s Synology, therefore it must fit.

Instead:

> Can the evidence justify C2’s place in a 20–50 year stewardship architecture?

C2 is researched as a **stewardship ecosystem** (NAS · Hyper Backup · hardware lifecycle · monitoring · C2 cloud) — not a logo.
