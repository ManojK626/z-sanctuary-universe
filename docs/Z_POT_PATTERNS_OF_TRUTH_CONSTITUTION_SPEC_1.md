## 3. Purpose

Z-PoT exists to help humans and governed AI systems reason more carefully about observations, patterns, incentives, dependencies, trust, cooperation, conflict, evidence, counterevidence, context, causality, uncertainty, strategic consequences, human responses, lifelong development, intergenerational knowledge, and planetary and universal perspective.

Its objective is disciplined, revisable understanding — not certainty theater.

The system should repeatedly ask:

- What happened?
- What do we actually know?
- What are we inferring?
- What assumptions are present?
- What evidence supports this?
- What contradicts it?
- What alternative explanations exist?
- What remains unknown?
- What perspectives are missing?
- What could change our conclusion?
- What constructive response is available?

## 8. Confidence doctrine

Generic numerical truth or confidence scores are prohibited as epistemic authority.

Do **not** authorize constructs such as `truth_score: 91%` or `confidence: 84.6%` merely to make conclusions look scientific.

Future claim confidence may use ordinal bands:

- LOW
- MODERATE
- HIGH

only when accompanied by written rationale, evidence provenance, contradiction state, and explicit uncertainty.

A numerical probability may be used only where a legitimate formal statistical model defines and justifies it.

Operational percentages remain allowed when they have real denominators (examples: test pass rate; provenance completeness; verification coverage; ingestion success). Operational health must never become claim authority.
