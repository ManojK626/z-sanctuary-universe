## Three permanent rules (every major project)

### Rule 1 — Freeze means freeze

Once architecture is declared **FROZEN**, changes occur **only** through a documented **Architecture Decision Record (ADR)** approved through governance (AMK gate, Merge Hold, 14 DRP where applicable).

No silent edits to locked layers. No contract expansion without ADR.

Infrastructure, governance, and reusable packages must **mature before** application-specific runtime code depends on them.

## Design philosophy (visible in the architecture)

| Principle | In practice |
| --------- | ----------- |

| AI **guides** rather than decides | AI Constitution v1 · human approval on insights |
| Consent **enables** rather than obstructs | Opt-in capture · withdrawal propagates |
| Architecture **creates clarity** rather than complexity | Three contract layers + handbook — separation of concerns |
