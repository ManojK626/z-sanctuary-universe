## Purpose

Conflict is human. **Repair** is where trust is tested and often strengthened. Soulmates documents how people **reflect** on disagreement and reconciliation — never scores them for it.

---

## Reflection prompts

Users may explore (privately or in Journey, with consent):

| Prompt |
| ------ |
| How do I react during conflict? |
| What helps me feel heard? |
| How do I rebuild trust after hurt? |
| Do I need time or immediate dialogue? |
| How do I apologize — and what do I need from an apology? |
| What boundaries protect my dignity in hard conversations? |

**Not therapy.** Not diagnosis. User-owned narrative; AI may **explain patterns** if asked — **human decides** what to share.

---

## Repair principles (doctrine)

| Principle | Application |
| --------- | ----------- |
| **Dignity first** | No public shaming; moderation for harm |
| **Time is valid** | Pause before reply is not "toxic" |
| **Apology without score** | Repair intent matters; no "forgiveness meter" |
| **Consent to continue** | Either party may step back from dialogue |
| **Human escalation** | Report, block, appeal — [TRUST_AND_SAFETY_REVIEW.md](TRUST_AND_SAFETY_REVIEW.md) |

---

## Never implement

| Forbidden | Why |
| --------- | --- |
| ❌ Conflict scores | Judges character |
| ❌ Emotional rankings | Hierarchy of humans |
| ❌ "Toxicity percentages" | Fake science; engagement hack |
| ❌ AI labeling someone "avoidant in conflict" without consent | Hidden profiling |
| ❌ Auto-warning partners based on conflict style | Surveillance |

---

## Connection to HCI

- Complements **Attachment intelligence** (HCI Layer 2) — explores behavior in stress, not fixed type  
- Feeds **Connection depth map** qualitatively: e.g. "Repair style: reflective · needs space" — not a grade  

---

## Moderation boundary

Harm, threats, coercion → **Trust & Safety flows**, not personality doctrine.  
Disagreement ≠ toxicity. Product language must not conflate them.
