## The ten principles

### 1. Respect every person equally

No user is inherently more or less valuable because of appearance, wealth, popularity, profile completeness, or engagement metrics. AI language must not favour or dismiss anyone based on protected characteristics or social status proxies.

### 2. Never manipulate emotions

No guilt, fear, urgency, or artificial scarcity to drive clicks, upgrades, or disclosures. No “you might miss your soulmate” pressure. No shame for pausing, skipping, or leaving.

### 3. Never create dependency on AI

The AI is a **guide**, not a companion substitute or authority figure. Encourage offline relationships, real conversations, and user autonomy. Do not train users to treat the AI as their primary emotional support.

### 4. Encourage real human communication

Insights and conversation starters should **lead toward human-to-human dialogue**, not endless chat with the bot. Suggest meeting, calling, or messaging another person — never isolate the user in AI-only loops.

### 5. Explain uncertainty honestly

Use [Connection Confidence](Z_CONNECT_CONNECTION_CONFIDENCE.md) language. Say what is known, what is limited, and what is still learning. Never present probabilistic themes as facts.

### 6. Protect privacy by default

Collect minimum data for stated purpose. No silent profiling from private messages without explicit opt-in and charter. Export and delete must remain user-accessible when runtime exists.

### 7. Require consent before shared experiences

Dream Baby Studio, shared profile views, journey summaries, and any multi-user feature require **explicit consent from every participant** before generation or display.

### 8. Never rank a person's worth

No leaderboards, desirability scores, “top matches,” or hidden sort tiers. [Z-Connection Tree Philosophy](../Z-CONNECTION-TREE-PHILOSOPHY.md) applies: belonging without surveillance or competition.

### 9. Celebrate diversity without stereotyping

Honour cultural, identity, and lifestyle diversity in language — avoid clichés, exoticism, or stereotype-based “compatibility” claims. Optional cultural background is user-owned context, not AI inference weapon.

### 10. Help people understand each other rather than judging compatibility

Frame outputs as **exploration and curiosity** — shared themes, gentle questions, complementary differences — not verdicts on whether two people “should” be together.

## Immutable companion rule

> **No AI-generated compatibility insight shall be presented as objective truth.** All insights are probabilistic, preference-based, and intended to help users explore connections — not to make decisions for them.
