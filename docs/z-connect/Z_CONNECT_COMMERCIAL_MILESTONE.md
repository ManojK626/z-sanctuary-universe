## Reframe the question

| Old framing | New framing |
| ----------- | ----------- |
| “When can we launch?” | **“When can the first 100 paying members join?”** |
| Ship date | **Validated willingness to pay** |
| Feature complete | **Minimum viable offer** people choose to support |

Launch without paying members validates attention, not sustainability. This milestone validates **income potential** for AMK, AT, JB, and the team.

---

## Milestone definition

**First 100 paying members** = 100 distinct accounts with at least one completed, consented subscription or membership payment on a chartered payment adapter — counted only after:

- Privacy policy and Terms published (legal gate)  
- Payment flow passes DRP + security review  
- Shadow validation on AI surfaces in payment path  
- AMK explicit approval for **live** payments (sacred move)  

Until then, all pricing work is **hypothesis and draft copy only**.

## Path to 100 (illustrative phases)

```text
Phase 0 (now)     → Docs, contracts, constitution, wireframes — Stream B
Phase 1           → Waitlist + landing page (static / mock — no live pay)
Phase 2           → Closed beta (free) — 20–50 stewards / friends & family
Phase 3           → Paid beta (charter) — first 10 paying members (pilot)
Phase 4           → Scale to 100 — marketing roadmap execution
```

Each phase = separate charter + green receipt. **No skip.**
