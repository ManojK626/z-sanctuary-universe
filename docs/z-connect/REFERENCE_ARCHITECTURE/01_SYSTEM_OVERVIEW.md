## What Z-Connect is

Z-Connect is a **human-centered relationship ecosystem** — friendships, romance, families, and communities — governed by the Z-Sanctuary stack with scientific integrity, consent, and human-first AI guidance.

**System ID:** Z-CONNECT-1 · **Parent:** Z-Sanctuary Universe · **Owner:** AMK-Goku

## What Z-Connect is not

- A destiny or compatibility oracle  
- A surveillance or ranking platform  
- A deployed product (yet)  
- An autonomous AI decision-maker  

```text
Documented ≠ Implemented ≠ Running
AI assists ≠ AI decides
Optional entertainment ≠ scientific prediction
```

Full boundaries: [Z_CONNECT_SYSTEM_BOUNDARIES.md](../Z_CONNECT_SYSTEM_BOUNDARIES.md)

## Mission (from charter)

Help people **explore connection** with clarity, consent, and compassion — using AI as a **guide**, never as authority.
