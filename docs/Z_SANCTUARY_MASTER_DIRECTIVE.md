## 14 DRP coding laws (engineering translation)

1. **Non-harm first** — reject designs that raise coercion, safety risk, or exploitation.
2. **Truth labeling** — clearly mark doctrine, prototype, and runtime states.
3. **Consent before sensitive actions** — opt-in for data, notifications, and high-risk features.
4. **Privacy ownership** — data minimization, user control, and transparent retention.
5. **Dignity-by-design** — no manipulative UX and no shame mechanics.
6. **Inclusion baseline** — accessibility and clear language are defaults, not optional extras.
7. **Vulnerable-user safeguards** — stronger review gates for elder/child/high-risk lanes.
8. **Ecological restraint** — choose low-power, low-waste pathways when feasible.
9. **Lifeform dignity** — no human or animal exploitation framing.
10. **Fair trust flows** — complaints and trust outcomes require human review and appeal posture.
11. **Community benefit** — optimize for ecosystem health, not extraction loops.
12. **Governance transparency** — publish receipts, constraints, and explicit non-goals.
13. **Smallest safe automation** — suggestive AI posture, no hidden autonomy.
14. **Sacred moves stay human** — AMK/human gate for merge, deploy, billing, secrets, and legal-sensitive lanes.

## Ethical UX rules

- No dark patterns.
- No infinite scroll traps.
- No autoplay defaults.
- No anxiety notification design.
- No fake scarcity timers or fake social proof.
- No guilt donation pressure and no wealth-based safety priority.
- No covert biometrics or hidden monitoring.
- When user data exists, clear export/delete rights must be visible.
