# Z-CIVD — Charter

**System ID:** Z-CIVD-0  
**Full name:** Creation · Vitality · Integrity · Dignity  
**Phase:** 0 — doctrine only  
**Status:** Turtle Mode — docs-first  
**Hub:** Z-Sanctuary Universe  
**Owner:** AMK-Goku

---

## Purpose

Z-CIVD is the **life-first creation doctrine** lane for Z-Sanctuary: how creative work, stewardship, labor energy, and community vitality are framed **before** any product, service, or public offer exists.

Phase 0 **does not** build runtime, provide coaching or therapy, or make health or productivity claims. It defines **boundaries and language** for adjacent awareness lanes (especially [Z-Nexus Engine](../z-nexus-engine/Z_NEXUS_ENGINE_0_FOUNDATION.md)).

---

## Concept

| Pillar | Meaning (Phase 0) |
| ------ | ----------------- |
| **Creation** | Honest making — art, craft, code, gardens, stories — without extractive hustle framing |
| **Vitality** | Sustainable human energy and dignity — **not** clinical wellness or medical advice |
| **Integrity** | Truthful scope; no false authority; Turtle Mode and human gate respected |
| **Dignity** | Workers, stewards, youth, and elders treated as people — not metrics |

---

## Scope

**In scope (Phase 0):**

- Charter and locked law (this document)
- Cross-links to Z-Nexus labor-energy and recovery nodes
- Privacy and safety boundaries for future static exhibits
- Readiness alignment with [Z-Strategist AI](../z-strategist-ai/README.md)

**Out of scope (Phase 0):**

- Apps, APIs, payments, subscriptions, or bookings
- Mental-health, coaching, counselling, or medical advice
- Productivity scoring, biometric tracking, or surveillance
- Public launch or deploy authority
- Company registration, tax, or banking conclusions

---

## Relationship to Z-Nexus Engine

Z-CIVD is **adjacent** to Z-Nexus resource and human-energy awareness:

- Nexus may illustrate **labor strain** and **community recovery** — Z-CIVD supplies the **life-first ethics** frame.
- Nexus mock nodes remain **non-authoritative**; Z-CIVD does not upgrade them to verified measurement.
