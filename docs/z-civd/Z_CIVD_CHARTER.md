# Z-CIVD Charter — Z Creation Intelligence & Living Design Engine

**Phase:** Z-CIVD-0  
**Status:** Doctrine only — Turtle Mode  
**Hub:** Z-Sanctuary Universe

---

## Full name

Z Creation Intelligence & Living Design Engine (Z-CIVD)

---

## Purpose

Transform a human idea, local resources, land, research, or design dream into a **responsible creation pathway** — researched, local-resource-aware, and ecosystem-protective — while protecting nature, animals, ecosystems, people, and future generations.

Z-CIVD does **not** build, certify, purchase, or deploy in Phase 0. It defines **how** creation should be thought about before any runtime exists.

---

## Scope

Any creation, design, version, invention, shelter, land use, tool, service, community project, or business concept that a human steward proposes — governed by life-first ethics and human review.

---

## Philosophy

| Principle | Meaning |
| --------- | ------- |
| **Human-first** | The creator’s dignity, safety, and informed consent come first |
| **Life-first** | Animals, ecosystems, water, soil, and future generations are not collateral |
| **Local reality** | Materials, skills, climate, and community context matter |
| **Stewardship** | Long-term repair, regeneration, and give-back are part of creation |

---

## Nature-first rule

- **Avoid animal-derived materials** where humane alternatives exist  
- **Avoid unnecessary extraction** — prefer what is already present, reclaimed, or regenerative  
- **Prefer** regenerative, reclaimed, recycled, low-impact, locally repairable resources  
- **Do not treat nature** as unlimited inventory  

---

## Safety rule

Z-CIVD may **educate, compare, plan, and simulate conceptually**.

Z-CIVD must **not** claim:

- Final engineering approval  
- Medical or health certification  
- Legal clearance  
- Structural sign-off  
- Verified environmental certification  

**Locked phrases:**

- Doctrine ≠ build permission  
- Simulation ≠ certified engineering  
- Harmony score ≠ legal or environmental approval  

---

## Human gate

**AMK-Goku / human approval** is required before any future build, runtime expansion, provider integration, map SDK, payment lane, or autonomous agent.

Phase 0 delivers **doctrine, architecture metadata, roadmap, and governance only**.
