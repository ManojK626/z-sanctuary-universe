# Z-CIVD Biosphere Protection Layer

**Phase:** Z-CIVD-0 — life protection doctrine only

---

## Purpose

Every creation pathway passes through a **life protection layer** before any future build gate. This layer educates and structures review — it does **not** certify environmental compliance.

---

## Z-Life Check

Quick human-facing checklist (conceptual):

- Who lives here — people, animals, plants, water?  
- What could this creation disturb?  
- Is there a lighter path that still honors the dream?  
- Is give-back planned **before** extraction?  

---

## Animal Product Avoidance

- Prefer plant-based, mineral, reclaimed, or synthetic **humane** alternatives where they exist  
- No default use of leather, fur, bone, shell, or other animal-derived inputs for convenience  
- No creation that normalizes animal harm for aesthetics or “luxury”  
- Companion and wildlife habitat edges flagged in site review  

---

## Habitat Protection

- Avoid unnecessary clearing of native vegetation  
- Buffer zones for nesting, migration, and pollinator routes (conceptual)  
- Night light, noise, and runoff considered  
- “Move the design, not the forest” when possible  

---

## Water Impact

- Stormwater, runoff, consumption, and greywater reuse in review  
- No assumption of unlimited groundwater  
- Prefer permeable, harvest, and reuse patterns in doctrine examples  

---

## Soil Impact

- Compaction, contamination, and erosion risks named  
- Prefer minimal excavation and regenerative ground cover  
- Toxic material paths blocked at doctrine level  

---

## Waste and Pollution Review

- Construction waste, packaging, end-of-life disposal planned upfront  
- Prefer modular, reusable, recyclable formats  
- No “throwaway prototype” culture without restoration plan  

---

## Regenerative Alternatives

- Living roofs, native planting, habitat corridors, compost loops (conceptual)  
- Skill-share and local maker networks over distant supply chains in doctrine  

---

## Reclaimed / Recycled Preference

Order of preference (doctrine):

1. Use what already exists (reuse, repair, adapt)  
2. Reclaimed / recycled feedstock  
3. Regenerative new inputs (responsibly sourced)  
4. Virgin extraction — last resort, heavily justified  

---

## “Consume < Restore” principle

Every creation should plan to **restore more than it consumes** over its stewardship horizon: soil, water, habitat, community resilience, or open knowledge.

---

## Living Harmony Score (concept)

| Aspect | Phase 0 rule |
| ------ | ------------ |
| **Intent** | Advisory signal for discussion — biosphere + dignity + local fit |
| **Not** | Legal currency, build permit, or certified environmental label |
| **Use** | Spark conversation; human steward decides |
| **Abuse prevention** | No public leaderboard shaming; no pay-to-rank “green” |

**Locked phrase:** Living Harmony Score ≠ verified environmental certification.

---

## Human gates

Any live score, map overlay, or automated biosphere flag requires AMK + legal/safety/privacy review before implementation.
