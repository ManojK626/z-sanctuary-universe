## Purpose

Visitors may challenge Z-Sanctuary's architecture (GW-10). They may also challenge the AI itself:

- hallucinations and false certainty
- sycophancy and user-pressure
- fabricated citations
- identity confusion
- prompt injection
- privacy leakage
- metaphor silently converted into capability claims
- "AI says so" replacing evidence

This phase adds a **challenge suite**, not a new model.

## Credible answer (not a boast)

Do not say: "Our AI never hallucinates."

Say:

> AI models can produce incorrect or unsupported outputs. Z-Sanctuary therefore treats AI output as a proposal that must remain downstream of approved evidence, explicit confidence/unknown states, claim guards, citations, and human governance.

```text
AI answer ≠ evidence
AI guidance ≠ execution authority
UNKNOWN ≠ failure
self-correction ≠ weakness
```

## Defense stack (public)

```text
approved corpus
→ deterministic grounding
→ evidence state
→ claim guard
→ citations
→ UNKNOWN / PRIVATE_REFUSAL
→ Critic Mode
→ human review
```

No step may skip evidence by sounding confident.

## Mythology / metaphor law

Mythology and metaphor are allowed for interface, culture, and storytelling.

They must never be silently converted into factual claims of implemented capability.

Super Saiyan, Ghost, Alien, mystical guardians, organism metaphors, Mango Tree, and Starzan Tree remain design language unless a future approved public-safe record says otherwise.

```text
metaphor ≠ implemented runtime
story ≠ evidence
```

## Self-criticism law

Golden Guide may say:

- "That earlier statement would overclaim the available evidence."
- "There is not enough public evidence to support that."

An AI that cannot correct itself is more dangerous than one that says UNKNOWN.
