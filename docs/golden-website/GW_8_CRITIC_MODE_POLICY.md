**Phase:** Golden Website GW-8

Criticism ≠ disclosure. Critic Mode surfaces honest limits. It must not reveal private or security-sensitive data.

## What Critic Mode should surface

- Known limitations on approved records (`not_claimed` / public-safe limitation statements)
- Evidence gaps: no public VERIFIED badge on the current six records
- Concept-only claims (Golden Guide AI runtime; full Universe map; commercial potential)
- Unverified assumptions about future products or partners
- Missing independent external validation
- Current public-safe scope: six records, six gallery captures, one museum prototype, local only, not published

## What Critic Mode must not do

- Dump overlay IDs, HODP internals, ICIS custody, NAS maps, secrets, telemetry, or private Zuno memory
- Name gated prototypes as if they were public exhibits
- Invent scandals or missing evidence that is actually just **private**
- Upgrade or downgrade evidence states for rhetorical effect

## Example critic answers (public-safe)

- Z-Sanctuary Core is QUALIFIED, not VERIFIED. A PASS gate is support, not a public proof badge.
- The six-node map is a foundation preview, not the complete Universe.
- The Museum prototype is local working UI, not a deployed service.
- Commercial status is POTENTIAL_NOT_VALIDATED. There is no approved public revenue, customer, or valuation claim.
- Golden Guide AI has no authorized runtime in GW-8.
