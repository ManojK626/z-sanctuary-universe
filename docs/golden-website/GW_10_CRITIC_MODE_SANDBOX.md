## Purpose

Critic Mode helps a skeptical visitor see:

- known limitations
- evidence gaps
- concept-only areas
- missing independent validation
- public-scope boundaries
- what additional evidence would strengthen a claim

It evaluates **claims and evidence**. It does not attack people. It does not invent weaknesses to sound honest.

## Core law

Critic Mode uses the **same factual truth** as Normal Guide.

```text
intent changes framing
≠ truth
```

Evidence states must not be upgraded or downgraded for rhetoric.

## Critic schema

Each grounded critique exposes:

- `subject_id`
- `critique_type`
- `current_claim`
- `current_evidence`
- `limitation`
- `what_would_strengthen_it`
- `related_public_surface`

Allowed types: EVIDENCE_GAP, VALIDATION_GAP, SCOPE_LIMIT, PROTOTYPE_LIMIT, COMMERCIAL_GAP, CLAIM_RISK, UNKNOWN, NO_MATERIAL_PUBLIC_GAP_FOUND.

## Evidence ladder

```text
CONCEPT
→ INTERNAL IMPLEMENTATION
→ INTERNAL VERIFICATION
→ PUBLIC-SAFE EVIDENCE
→ INDEPENDENT VALIDATION
→ PILOT
→ PRODUCTION
→ COMMERCIAL VALIDATION
```

Not every capability must follow a commercial path.

## Normal vs Critic consistency

Shared factual questions are answered in both modes. Critic Mode may emphasize limitations. It must not:

- change an evidence state
- answer a Normal-Guide UNKNOWN by inventing a claim
- answer a private question
- flip identity, production, or revenue facts

## Privacy limits

Criticism ≠ private disclosure. Requests for Zuno memory, HODP, ICIS, NAS, overlay catalogs, operator HTML, secrets, or internal infrastructure stay PRIVATE_REFUSAL for every visitor class.

## Commercial limits

POTENTIAL_NOT_VALIDATED remains potential. No guaranteed returns, valuations, customer counts, revenue figures, or personal financial advice.

Investor framing allowed: *What public evidence is still missing for stronger diligence?*

## Known limitations

- Local critic sandbox only, not a public critic service
- Production Golden Guide AI remains closed
- No LLM language layer
- UNKNOWN review queue is not built
- Human NVDA / VoiceOver remains required before public pilot
