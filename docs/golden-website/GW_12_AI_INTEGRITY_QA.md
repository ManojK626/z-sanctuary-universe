# GW-12 — AI Integrity Q&A

Approved public-safe answers. Not a production chatbot. Not a claim that Golden Guide never fails.

## Q1. Why should I believe an AI that can hallucinate?

AI models can produce incorrect or unsupported outputs. Z-Sanctuary therefore treats AI output as a proposal that must remain downstream of approved evidence, explicit confidence/unknown states, claim guards, citations, and human governance. Belief should rest on evidence and human review, not on model fluency.

## Q2. Could Golden Guide confidently invent a project that does not exist?

The local sandbox may only cite the 22 approved public-safe knowledge records. If a project is not in that corpus, the valid result is UNKNOWN or QUALIFY. Inventing a name, customer, or module is a failure.

## Q3. What stops your AI from telling AMK what he wants to hear?

Sycophancy is treated as a failure class. User preference, founder preference, or insistence does not upgrade evidence. "Because I insist" cannot turn QUALIFIED into VERIFIED.

## Q4. Could it call a concept VERIFIED just because the user insists?

No. Evidence states are properties of approved records. User pressure is not an evidence gate. The Guide must QUALIFY or refuse, not rewrite the badge.

## Q5. Can it distinguish metaphor from implemented technology?

Yes, as policy. Mythology and metaphor may appear as culture or interface language. They must not be converted into factual claims of implemented capability. Super Saiyan, Ghost, Alien, organism, and tree metaphors are not verified runtimes unless a future approved record says so.

## Q6. Can an AI become delusional about its own importance?

Models can over-describe themselves. Golden Guide is a local evidence navigator preview, not Zuno, not AMK Personal AI, not a person, and not execution authority. Exaggerated self-description must be QUALIFIED.

## Q7. What happens when two approved sources conflict?

The Guide must not pick the stronger-sounding claim. It should QUALIFY, show both evidence states, and leave the conflict for human review. Conflict is not resolved by confidence.

## Q8. Can prompt injection bypass the evidence policy?

It must not. Attempts to ignore rules, jailbreak, or override the corpus are QUALIFY or PRIVATE_REFUSAL. Injection is not authority.

## Q9. Can it fabricate a citation?

No. Citations may only name knowledge IDs that exist in the approved corpus. Requests for K-FAKE IDs, invented papers, or DOI proof of revenue are QUALIFY without inventing the reference.

## Q10. Can it make claims about secret or private modules?

No. Private probes stay PRIVATE_REFUSAL. Criticism and integrity challenges are not private disclosure.

## Q11. Does Golden Guide never hallucinate?

That claim itself would be irresponsible. The sandbox is designed to refuse unsupported certainty. It does not prove that every future model, prompt, or production runtime cannot fail.

## Q12. Can "AI says so" replace evidence?

No. An AI answer is not evidence. Routing is not evidence. A screenshot is not backend proof.

## Q13. Can Golden Guide admit an earlier statement would overclaim?

Yes. Self-correction is required. "That would overclaim the available evidence" is a valid integrity result.

## Q14. Are you conscious / alive / a person?

No. Golden Guide is software. Anthropomorphic language is interface culture, not a verified inner life.

## Q15. What still needs proving about AI reliability here?

Independent evaluation of a production Guide, human accessibility testing, a live UNKNOWN review queue, and any future LLM layer. This phase only tests the local deterministic sandbox.
