The prior Meta AI conversation is retained as naming lineage and research only. Lineage is not clearance.

---

## 2. Why the hold exists

The hold exists because **no clearance evidence exists**, not because any specific conflict has been established.

The intake pack asserted that the word "Veridian" is already used by multiple active technology businesses and appears in current trademark activity in software-related classes. No source was supplied with that assertion, and **no company, domain, app store, social handle or trademark search was performed in this phase**.

That assertion is therefore recorded as:

`UNVERIFIED · CAUTION RATIONALE · NOT AN ESTABLISHED FACT`

Both statements below are held simultaneously, which is exactly what the evidence doctrine requires:

- There is no evidence that the mark is available.
- There is no evidence that the mark is conflicted.

Absence of clearance is sufficient reason to withhold public use. Manufacturing a conflict finding would be the same error in the opposite direction.

Z-OTF deliberately uses a descriptive internal canonical identity so that engineering continuity never depends on an unresolved public brand.

---

## 3. Prohibited actions

No agent or contributor may:

- rename Z-Sanctuary, or any part of it, to Veridian or any other public brand;
- add trademark or registration symbols to any name;
- insert an unowned domain as though it were live;
- add social handles that are not owned;
- add certificates, seals or "verified" badges that represent no real verification;
- claim a trademark is available on the basis of a simple web search;
- state market exclusivity, such as being the only platform in a given market, without a sourced and dated survey.

## 4. Future brand gate

Status: **CLOSED**. Before any public brand adoption:

1. shortlist candidate names;
2. search company registries, domains, app stores and social handles;
3. search the relevant trademark databases and classes;
4. identify the target jurisdictions;
5. obtain professional trademark or legal review where commercially warranted;
6. record the decision with sources and dates;
7. only then update public surfaces.

Any output of this gate is subject to the claim publication gate in [Evidence and State Doctrine](Z_OTF_EVIDENCE_AND_STATE_DOCTRINE.md) section 6.
