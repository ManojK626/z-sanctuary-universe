## 4. Purpose

Z-OTF may eventually coordinate the following, each only under a separate future gate:

1. **Operational communication** — structured messages, task handoffs, customer and vendor communication, human-approved drafts.
2. **Knowledge access** — local-first documents, source-linked answers, declared provenance and freshness.
3. **Operational work** — task states, human assignments, evidence of completion.
4. **Operational Provenance Records (`OPR`)** — source references, work state, timestamps, actors, custody and verification metadata, canonical payloads, and digests or signatures when later authorized. An OPR is operational provenance, not legal evidence.
5. **Commercial evidence** — comparisons, pricing posture, cost-to-serve, claims and readiness, coordinated with Z-SUSBV rather than replacing it.
6. **Learning and feedback** — user feedback, lessons and improvement proposals, governed by Z-LIC principles, with no silent authority escalation.
7. **Human stewardship** — observe, verify, suggest; the human decides.

## 7. Why Z-OTF stays inside Z-Sanctuary

The parent repository already contains the evidence, governance, commercial and learning primitives Z-OTF needs. The [Reuse and Overlap Matrix](Z_OTF_REUSE_AND_OVERLAP_MATRIX.md) records the located surfaces.

Creating Z-OTF as an independent architecture now would cause duplicated registries, competing evidence states, competing governance, commercial-policy drift, inconsistent receipts, separate awareness planes, conflicting minibot authority, duplicated dashboards, and increased recovery and custody burden.

Therefore:

> Reuse the organism; specialize the product lane.

Z-OTF may later become separately deployable while still inheriting Z-Sanctuary governance and contracts. That remains a future decision, gated in the roadmap.
