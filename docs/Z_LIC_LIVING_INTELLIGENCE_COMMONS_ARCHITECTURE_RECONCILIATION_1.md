## F. Z-Omni / Fusion Mapping

Z-OMNI on this base is a **Cursor identity/manifest** (`docs/Z-OMNI-CURSOR-UPGRADE-MANIFEST.md`, `.cursor/rules/z-omni-unified-organism.mdc`): one-with-core, §9 completions, 14 DRP compassion. It is **not** a multi-AI deliberation council.

`scripts/z_fusion_council.mjs` is a **read-only weighted-vote synthesizer** over health reports. It emits `consensus`, `confidence`, `conflicts`, and a single `final_recommendation`. Nearby votes within 60% of the top score are listed as `conflicts`, but minority **arguments are not preserved**. Consensus ratio is mapped to high/medium/low confidence.

**Architectural risk:** Fusion can look like epistemic authority. That **CONFLICTS** with candidate doctrine “Consensus is not truth.”

Z-LIC must not promote Fusion Council into a truth oracle. Future composition should keep Fusion as **advisory synthesis with mandatory dissent fields**, or route deliberation around it.

GGAESP `core_engine/browser/ggaesp_pipeline.js` folds `minorityWarning` into a numeric score. That is **CONFLICTING** with dissent-as-first-class-text: minority becomes a weight, not a preserved view.
