## Allowed uses (internal)

Z Formula may guide:

- internal reasoning and architecture reviews
- ecosystem and module **evaluation** (metadata, docs, registries)
- ethical scoring vocabulary for reports
- fusion **reasoning** (with Z-FUSION; human decides)
- approval-tier **support** (with Z-ATE; not replacement)
- topology and isolation judgments (with Z-ECO-MAP)
- human-centered outcome framing in dashboards (signals, not execution)

## Forbidden uses

Z Formula must **not** be used to claim or imply:

- supernatural or guaranteed prediction
- public “secret formula” sale or exposure of protected internals
- guaranteed ROI, lottery, or financial certainty
- medical, legal, or clinical certainty
- autonomous authority over merge, deploy, or payment
- bypass of AMK approval or 14 DRP
- replacement of [Z_PAYMENT_OWNERSHIP_AND_BUSINESS_AI_GOVERNANCE.md](Z_PAYMENT_OWNERSHIP_AND_BUSINESS_AI_GOVERNANCE.md) or legal ops lanes
